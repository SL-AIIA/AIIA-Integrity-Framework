\# 2\_JE Extension \- Junior Edition (v1.0)

\*\*Purpose\*\*:    
A simple browser extension that helps users protect their real intention when chatting with AI tools.

\#\#\# Current Status  
Early prototype (v1.0). Designed to be simple and easy to test.

\#\#\# How to Install (For Non-Technical Users)

1\. Download this entire repository as a ZIP file.  
2\. Unzip the folder on your computer.  
3\. Open Chrome or Edge browser.  
4\. Go to this address: \`chrome://extensions/\`  
5\. Turn on \*\*Developer Mode\*\* (switch at the top right).  
6\. Click \*\*"Load unpacked"\*\*.  
7\. Select the folder named \`2\_JE\_Extension\`.  
8\. The extension should now be active.

You should see a small \*\*🔒 Anchor\*\* button appear when you are on Grok, ChatGPT, or Claude.

\---

\*\*How to Use\*\*

1\. Go to any AI chat (Grok, ChatGPT, Claude, etc.)  
2\. Type your request normally.  
3\. Click the \*\*🔒 Anchor\*\* button that appears.  
4\. Confirm your intent in the popup.  
5\. Send your message to the AI.  
6\. (Later) Click \*\*🔍 Check Reply\*\* to see if the AI followed your original intention.
